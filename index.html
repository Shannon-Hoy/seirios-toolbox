Ocean Mapping Toolbox

Seirios Solutions, LLC
Free browser tools for planning and preparing ocean mapping work. Open one and it runs in your browser, so the files you load stay on your machine and never get uploaded. Once a tool has loaded you can carry on working offline.

Planning

Questions you answer before you sail: where the vessel can work productively, how long a survey takes, and what it costs.

Data utilities

Smaller jobs on files you already have: masking, repairing, reprojecting, converting.
