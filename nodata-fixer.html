NoData Fixer

Seirios Ocean Mapping Toolbox
Fix uninterpretable NoData values and export the repaired raster as a GeoTIFF or Arc ASCII grid. Some sources write empty cells as 0 instead of a proper NoData flag: multibeam mosaics pulled with Extract Grid in the NCEI Bathymetry Viewer, for example, which then render as a flat sea-level plane in Fledermaus. Replaces the Global Mapper export step in the Data Preparation SOP. Your rasters are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Output coordinate system:
UTM zone to EPSG (the usual case): WGS 84 north = 326 + zone, south = 327 + zone. Zone
Other standards: 4326 WGS 84 geographic · 3857 Web Mercator · 3031 Antarctic · 3995 Arctic Polar Stereographic. Zone from longitude: floor((lon + 180) / 6) + 1.
Reprojection resamples by nearest neighbour and squares the cells for Arc ASCII. Fine for masks and quicklooks; for survey deliverables prefer the source CRS.
NODATA_value written to file: 0 matches the SOP / Global Mapper settings. -9999 is the common GIS convention; both work in Fledermaus.
